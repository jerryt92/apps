关于照片图库、照片地图、实况照片与地图轨迹的常见问题。Answers about the photo library, photo map, Live Photos and Map Track.

常见问题Frequently asked questions

为什么看不到我的照片？Why can't I see my photos?

浏览照片需要照片图库权限。首次打开时可以选择「允许完全访问」或「限制访问」；如果之前选了「不允许」，请到系统设置 → PhotoAssistant → 照片中更改。选择「限制访问」时，应用只显示你勾选的照片。Browsing requires photo library permission. On first launch you can choose Full Access or Limited Access; if you previously chose Don't Allow, change it in Settings → PhotoAssistant → Photos. With Limited Access, the app only shows the photos you selected.

照片地图上为什么没有照片？Why is the photo map empty?

只有包含位置信息的照片才会出现在地图上。拍摄时开启相机的定位权限，之后拍的照片就会带上位置；已有照片若没有位置信息，则不会显示。Only photos that contain location data appear on the map. Enable location for the Camera app so new photos carry coordinates; existing photos without location data won't be shown.

如何制作实况照片？How do I make a Live Photo?

进入「功能 → 实况照片制作」，从相册或暂存区选择一段视频。封面默认取第一帧，可以打开封面编辑器在时间轴上逐帧挑选，确认后生成并保存到相册。Go to Tools → Live Photo Maker and pick a video from your library or the staging area. The cover defaults to the first frame; open the cover editor to choose any frame on the timeline, then compose and save to your library.

地图轨迹需要什么素材？What does Map Track need?

选择一趟行程中拍摄、带位置信息的照片和视频。应用会按拍摄时间把它们在地图上逐个播放，你可以播放、暂停或回到起点，并导出 3D 动画视频保存到相册或直接分享。Pick geotagged photos and videos from one trip. The app plays them on the map in capture order; you can play, pause or restart, and export a 3D animation video to save or share.

暂存区是什么？What is the staging area?

暂存区是从「文件」App 导入素材的中转站。把视频或照片加入暂存区后，实况照片制作等工具可以直接选用，无需先存进相册。The staging area holds files imported from the Files app. Once added, tools like Live Photo Maker can use them directly without saving to your library first.

我的照片会被上传吗？Are my photos uploaded?

不会。浏览、地图展示、实况照片与轨迹影片都在设备本地完成，应用不上传照片，也不需要账号。详情见隐私说明。No. Browsing, map display, Live Photos and track movies all happen locally on your device. The app never uploads photos and requires no account. See the privacy page for details.
