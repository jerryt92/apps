应用用途What the app does

PhotoAssistant 用于浏览照片图库、在地图上查看带位置的照片、把视频制作成实况照片，以及把一趟行程的媒体导出为地图轨迹影片。地图显示由 Apple MapKit 提供。PhotoAssistant browses your photo library, shows geotagged photos on a map, composes Live Photos from videos and exports trip media as map track movies. Map display is provided by Apple MapKit.

照片图库Photo library

应用需要照片图库权限才能浏览和创作。你可以选择「限制访问」，只授权部分照片；应用只读取你允许的内容。生成实况照片或保存轨迹影片时，应用会请求向相册添加内容的权限。所有处理都在设备本地完成，照片不会上传到任何服务器。The app needs photo library permission to browse and create. You can choose Limited Access and grant only selected photos; the app reads only what you allow. When composing Live Photos or saving track movies, it requests permission to add items to your library. All processing happens locally on your device; photos are never uploaded to any server.

位置信息Location

照片地图读取的是照片自带的位置信息（拍摄时写入的 GPS 坐标），用于把照片展示在地图上。应用不会持续追踪你的实时位置。你可以在系统设置中管理或撤销相关授权。The photo map reads the location embedded in your photos (GPS coordinates written at capture time) to place them on the map. The app does not continuously track your real-time location. You can manage or revoke permissions in system Settings.

保存在设备上的数据Data saved on your device

应用在设备本地保存偏好设置（如照片排序方式、地图底图样式）以及你加入暂存区的文件引用。这些数据不随账号同步（应用没有账号），卸载应用即删除。The app stores preferences (such as photo sort order and map style) and references to files you add to the staging area locally on your device. Nothing syncs to an account — there is none — and uninstalling the app removes it.

第三方服务Third-party services

地图渲染由 Apple MapKit 提供，可能受 Apple 隐私政策的约束。应用不接入广告或第三方分析服务。Map rendering is provided by Apple MapKit and may be subject to Apple's privacy policy. The app includes no advertising or third-party analytics.
