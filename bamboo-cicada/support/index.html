技术支持

如果竹知了没有声音、震动反馈不明显，或摇动后没有反应，请先确认系统声音、静音模式、震动设置和动作权限是否可用。

常见问题

没有声音：请调高媒体音量，关闭静音限制，并确认设备没有连接到不可用的蓝牙音频设备。

没有震动：请在系统设置中开启触感反馈。部分 iPad 或模拟器不支持与 iPhone 相同的震动效果。

摇动不灵敏：请握稳设备并轻轻做圆周摇动，竹知了会跟随持续的节奏变化。

Support

If Bamboo Cicada: Chinese Folk Sound Toy has no sound, weak haptics, or does not react to shaking, first check media volume, silent mode, haptic settings, and motion availability.

Common Questions

No sound: Raise media volume, check silent restrictions, and make sure audio is not routed to an unavailable Bluetooth device.

No haptics: Enable haptic feedback in system settings. Some iPad models or simulators do not support the same vibration effects as iPhone.

Shake feels insensitive: Hold the device steadily and move it in a gentle circular rhythm. The toy responds best to sustained motion.
