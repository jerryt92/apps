LunaScope

隐私政策Privacy Policy

本政策说明 LunaScope 在连接相机、保存拍摄设置和分享诊断时如何处理数据。This policy explains how LunaScope handles data when you connect a camera, save capture settings and share diagnostics.

连接相机与本地存储Camera connection and on-device storage

LunaScope 使用蓝牙发现并配对相机，通过本地网络传输实时画面、控制命令及相机媒体。相机授权资料和已保存的相机 Wi-Fi 凭据保存在系统钥匙串。拍摄参数预设、云台位置和应用偏好保存在设备上。LunaScope uses Bluetooth to discover and pair with your camera. Live view, camera commands and camera media travel over the local network. Camera authorization records and saved camera Wi-Fi credentials are kept in the system Keychain. Capture presets, gimbal positions and app preferences are stored on your device.

照片和视频Photos and videos

当你选择将相机媒体保存到系统「照片」时，LunaScope 使用「照片」权限查找或创建 LunaScope 相簿，并保存你选中的照片或视频。你可在系统设置中管理权限。When you save camera media to Photos, LunaScope uses Photos permission to find or create the LunaScope album and save the files you selected. You can manage permissions in system Settings.

诊断与技术支持Diagnostics and support

诊断日志保存在设备上，应用启动时会清理超出七天保留范围的日志。诊断归档仅在你手动请求时生成，由你选择分享对象。若你通过邮件联系开发者，邮件地址、内容和所附文件会用于处理该请求。Diagnostic logs stay on your device. The app removes logs outside the seven-day retention window when it starts. A diagnostics archive is created when you request one, and you choose where to share it. If you contact the developer by email, your address, message and attachments are used to respond to your request.
