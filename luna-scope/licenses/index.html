OpenCine / LunaScopeTransport

LunaScope 使用 OpenCine 实现相机连接、拍摄参数控制、实时取景和相册缩略图处理。LunaScopeTransport 在其相机适配代码基础上接入影石360 Luna 系列相机。LunaScope uses OpenCine for camera connections, shooting controls, live preview, and album thumbnails. LunaScopeTransport adapts its camera integration for Insta360 Luna series cameras.
