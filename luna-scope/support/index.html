技术支持Support

LunaScope 支持 Insta360 Luna 系列相机，可在 iPhone 和 iPad 上使用。LunaScope supports Insta360 Luna series cameras on iPhone and iPad.

发现和连接相机Find and connect your camera

打开相机和设备的蓝牙，在 LunaScope 的「连接」页选择附近的相机。按系统提示允许蓝牙和本地网络访问，并加入相机网络。Turn on your camera and enable Bluetooth on your device. Choose a nearby camera in the Connect tab, then follow the system prompts to allow Bluetooth and local network access and join the camera network.

保存和恢复云台位置Save and restore a gimbal position

相机空闲时，在拍摄页用摇杆调整构图，然后点「记忆云台位置」。需要回到上次保存的角度时，点「恢复上次位置」。如果 App 提示翻转方向不一致，请先按提示恢复翻转方向。With the camera idle, frame your shot with the joystick in the Capture tab and save the gimbal position. Use the restore control to return to the last saved angle. If the app reports a different flip orientation, match the saved orientation first.

连续拍摄时云台自动归位Auto-return the gimbal during continuous shooting

长时间连续拍摄时，云台可能缓慢偏移，画面会跟着一点点移位。拍摄模式选「连续拍摄」后，「云台位置」菜单里会出现「连续拍摄时云台自动归位」，默认勾选。勾选后，开始连续拍摄的那一刻 App 会记下云台角度，之后每张拍摄前先让云台回到这个角度，停稳再拍。这个角度只用于本次连续拍摄，不会覆盖手动记忆的位置。Over a long continuous shoot the gimbal can drift slowly, and the framing shifts with it. With the shooting mode set to Continuous, the Gimbal position menu shows “Auto-return gimbal while shooting”, which is on by default. When it is on, the app remembers the gimbal angle at the moment you start continuous shooting, then returns the gimbal to that angle and waits for it to settle before every photo. The angle is used for that shoot only and does not replace your manually saved position.

用连续拍摄做定点延时时，建议保持勾选，并把相机固定在三脚架上。Keep it on when you shoot a fixed-position time-lapse with continuous shooting, and mount the camera on a tripod.

联系开发者Contact the developer

反馈问题时，请提供相机型号、固件版本、系统版本和复现步骤。也可以在「设置」的高级设置页手动生成诊断文件并随邮件发送。When reporting an issue, include your camera model, firmware, OS version and the steps to reproduce it. You can also create a diagnostics archive in Advanced Settings and attach it to your email.
