关于地图、对比、搜索与定位的常见问题。Answers about maps, comparison, search and location.

常见问题Frequently asked questions

如何开启双图对比？How do I compare two maps?

点击地图左下角菜单，进入“对比模式”，打开对比开关。可交换上下地图，并选择是否保持比例尺上下一致。Open the lower-left map menu, choose Compare mode and turn it on. You can swap the panes and choose whether their scales stay synchronized.

搜索或定位会移动哪张地图？Which map receives a search or location?

对比模式下，先点击目标地图。带强调色边框的面板是当前面板，之后发起的搜索或定位会作用于该面板。In comparison mode, tap the target map first. The pane with the accent border is active; a new search or location request targets that pane.

为什么首次打开没有询问定位权限？Why is there no location prompt at launch?

地图浏览和地点搜索不要求你提供当前位置。只有点击定位按钮时才请求权限并获取一次位置；不会持续追踪。Browsing maps and searching places do not require your current location. Permission and a one-time fix are requested when you tap Locate; there is no continuous tracking.

定位失败怎么办？What if location fails?

检查系统定位服务，以及系统设置中 MapLab 的位置权限。可在 App 的设置 → 权限检查中查看状态；网络与设备环境也可能影响定位。Check system Location Services and MapLab’s location permission in system Settings. You can also check the status under Settings → Permissions in the app. Connectivity and device conditions may affect location.

如何只看卫星影像？How do I view satellite imagery without labels?

在地图设置中切换到卫星底图，然后关闭卫星道路与地名标注。还可按需调整交通、兴趣点和比例尺显示。Choose the satellite base map in map settings, then turn off satellite roads and labels. Traffic, points of interest and scale visibility can also be adjusted.

如何删除搜索历史？How do I delete search history?

在搜索页的最近记录中左滑删除单条，或点击“全部清除”。应用最多保存最近 10 条搜索记录。Swipe left on a recent search to delete it, or choose Clear All. The app saves up to 10 recent searches.
