应用用途What the app does

MapLab 用于地图浏览、双图对比、地点搜索与地图显示设置。地图及搜索功能通过 Apple MapKit 提供。MapLab provides map browsing, comparison, place search and display preferences. Maps and search are provided through Apple MapKit.

位置信息Location

仅当你点击定位按钮时，应用才请求定位授权并获取一次当前位置，用于移动地图视野。应用不会持续追踪位置。你可以在系统设置中管理或撤销授权；不授权仍可浏览地图和搜索地点。The app requests location permission and a one-time location fix when you tap Locate, to move the map view. It does not continuously track your location. Manage or revoke permission in system Settings; browsing and place search remain available without it.

保存在设备上的数据Data saved on your device

应用在设备本地保存地图视野、底图和图层偏好、对比模式设置，以及最多 10 条最近搜索记录。搜索记录包含地点名称、地址与坐标，可在搜索页逐条删除或全部清除。恢复默认设置可重置应用偏好。The app stores map views, base map and layer preferences, comparison settings and up to 10 recent searches locally on your device. Search history includes place names, addresses and coordinates; delete entries individually or clear them all on the search screen. Restore Defaults resets app preferences.

地图服务与网络请求Map services and network requests

浏览地图和搜索地点需要向 Apple 地图服务发送相关请求，例如当前地图范围或你输入的搜索内容。相关处理遵循 Apple 的隐私政策。MapLab 没有用于接收用户地图或搜索数据的自建后端。Browsing and search send relevant requests, such as the current map region or entered search text, to Apple Maps services. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that processing. MapLab does not operate a backend for receiving your map or search data.

你主动发送的反馈Feedback you send

如果你通过邮件联系开发者，邮件地址、正文与附件会用于处理你的问题。请避免发送与问题无关的敏感位置或个人信息。If you email the developer, your email address, message and attachments are used to address your request. Avoid sending sensitive locations or personal information unrelated to the issue.
